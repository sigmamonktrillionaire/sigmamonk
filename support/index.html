Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

In the app, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. It's also in the Armory.

Make sure your iPhone is signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In the app, go to Settings → Manage subscription. You can also go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why isn't app blocking working?

Blocking apps is part of Pro. Without Pro, focus sessions run on the honor system.

With Pro, the app needs Screen Time access, which it asks for on the Focus screen. If you turned access off, go to iPhone Settings → Screen Time, then come back to Focus and tap Allow Screen Time access.

What are streak freezes?

A freeze covers a missed day so your streak survives.

You start with one, and you earn another at every 7 days of streak while you have fewer than two. You can buy more in the Armory.

The app only uses freezes automatically when you have enough to cover every missed day. You can turn that off in Settings.

How do I switch protocols?

Open the protocol you want. You can queue it to start the day after your current one ends, which keeps your streak going, or hold the switch button to change right away.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no cloud sync.

Your data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, so restoring a backup brings it back. Deleting the app erases it, so save a copy first from Settings → Export my data.

For details, see the Privacy Policy.

How does the Sigma League work?

The Sigma League is a weekly leaderboard on Apple's Game Center. Join in the Armory, then sign in to Game Center when the app asks.

Every day you complete earns 100 points plus a bonus for your streak, partial days earn some points, and the board starts fresh each week.

How do I change my reminders?

Go to Settings → Reminders to turn the mindset drop, streak alerts and Monday recap on or off.
