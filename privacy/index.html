Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version: Sigma Monk Trillionaire doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you enter stays on your iPhone, and you can export or erase it at any time.

Who we are

Sigma Monk Trillionaire ("the app") is made by an independent developer ("we" or "us"). If you have a question about this policy, email sigmamonktrillionaire@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't send your information to us, and we don't run servers that receive it. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we never sell or share personal information.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter in the app is stored only on your iPhone:

  • your protocols, habits, streaks, Discipline Score and focus sessions
  • your journal entries
  • your Wealth Stack: income goals, side hustles, revenue, hours and net worth
  • your profile: name, @handle and "your why"
  • your settings

Only the app and its built-in Screen Time extensions can read this data. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes it, and Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

Apple services the app uses

Screen Time

If you turn on app blocking, the app uses Apple's Screen Time to block the apps you choose. Apple gives the app anonymous tokens for those apps, so the app can't tell which apps you picked or see how you use your phone. None of this is sent to us.

Purchases

Apple processes all purchases and subscriptions, and we never see your payment details. The app receives confirmation from Apple of what you've bought so it can unlock it.

Game Center (Sigma League members only)

If you join the Sigma League and sign in to Game Center, the app posts your weekly league points and your best Discipline Score to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Other players see them next to your Game Center name. Scores stop being posted if your league membership ends or you sign out of Game Center.

Notifications

Reminders, such as the daily mindset drop, streak alerts and the Monday recap, are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server, and you can turn each one off in the app's Settings.

Apple's Privacy Policy covers these services.

Sharing

Nothing leaves the app unless you tap share. A share card shows things like your Discipline Score, streak, rank and protocol day, plus your @handle if you've set one. It goes only to the app you choose, such as Instagram or Messages, and that app's privacy policy applies. When you share to Instagram Stories, Instagram also receives an identifier for our app, so it knows where the share came from.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Export my data saves a copy of everything as a file.
  • Erase: Settings → Erase all data deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Delete the app: this removes its data from your iPhone. Copies in your backups follow your backup settings.
  • Screen Time access: turn it off in iPhone Settings → Screen Time.
  • Reminders: turn them off in the app's Settings, or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for us to hand over, correct or delete. If you have a question, email us.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
